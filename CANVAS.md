12. **Sesi Santuy (Bongkar Kedok):** Early dinner & Guardian Angel stealth reveal + SFX misteri.
13. **Grand Awarding Stage:** 4 trofi & kartu juara (Olympic, Costume, Lunch, Most Entertaining) + photo slot & confetti.
